A better embedder beats a better reranker

Six retrieval pipelines on the same customer-support knowledge base, scored on whether the right document lands in the top 3. Swapping the embedding model moves the number roughly twice as far as bolting a reranker onto the old one — and the gap widens exactly where it hurts, on hard questions.
